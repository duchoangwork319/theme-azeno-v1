Which Fusion tri suit is best for a first 70.3?+

TEMPO! TWO is usually the most balanced starting point for athletes who want practical storage, UPF 50+ coverage and a comfortable race fit. TEMPO! THREE is a strong option for athletes prioritising a more approachable fit.

What is the difference between TEMPO! PRO, ONE, TWO and THREE?+

PRO prioritises elite aerodynamics, ONE targets competitive age-groupers, TWO balances speed with storage and comfort, and THREE focuses on accessible race-day confidence.

Which tri suit is best for hot and humid races?+

The right choice depends on your preference for fit, sun coverage, fabric characteristics and nutrition storage. TEMPO! ONE, TWO and THREE offer UPF 50+ coverage, while TEMPO! PRO offers UPF 35+ with a more aggressive aerodynamic focus.

Should a Fusion tri suit feel tight?+

Yes, especially performance-oriented models. It should feel secure without restricting breathing, shoulder movement or your ability to hold the cycling position.

Where can I carry gels and race nutrition?+

TEMPO! PRO and ONE use internal storage, while TWO and THREE provide more accessible external or leg-pocket storage. Confirm exact pocket configuration for the selected model.

Is there a women-specific Fusion tri suit?+

Yes. 5DLA currently carries the Women’s TEMPO! ONE Tri Suit, designed around a women-specific race fit.

How do I choose my size?+

Use your actual body measurements and consult the Fusion size guide. Contact 5DLA if you are between sizes.
