HOW TO CHOOSE MEN’S PERFORMANCE CLOTHING

Danish engineering, European production

FUSION designs its sportswear in Denmark and produces through its own European manufacturing set-up, using high-quality European fabrics and close control over construction. Across selected garments, details include circular-knit Italian fabric, soft flatlock seams and STANDARD 100 by OEKO-TEX® certification. The result is premium kit built for repeated training and washing—not disposable seasonal sportswear.

Shop by sport, then by session

Start with the activity you train most often. Running tops and tights prioritise freedom of movement, cycling apparel is shaped for the riding position, and triathlon suits combine swim, bike and run requirements. Gym and HYROX clothing should move comfortably through running, sled work and functional strength sessions.

Build a hot-weather training rotation

For Hong Kong, Singapore and Southeast Asia, lightweight layers and fast-drying fabrics make repeat training easier. Choose enough tops, shorts and socks to rotate through humid sessions, and add sun-protective accessories for exposed outdoor training. Product fit varies, so use the Fusion size guide before ordering.

Accessories complete the system

Calf sleeves, aero socks, Cool Wings and other accessories can support a specific race or training setup. Use product-type filters to compare accessories separately, then check each product page for current colours, sizes and technical specifications.
