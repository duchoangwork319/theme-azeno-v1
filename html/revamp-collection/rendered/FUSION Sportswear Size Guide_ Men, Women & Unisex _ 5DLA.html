FUSION SPORTSWEAR

Measure once.
Choose with confidence.

Use a soft tape measure over light clothing. Keep it level and comfortably close to the body, without pulling tight. Measure the fullest part of the chest or bust and hip, and the natural waistline.

FUSION is engineered in Denmark and made in Europe for movement. Its technical stretch fabrics and close athletic cutting are designed to sit cleanly against the body, manage moisture and reduce excess fabric during endurance sport.

FUSION FIT FOR ASIA

Technical fit for heat, humidity and long training days.

For runners and riders in Hong Kong, Singapore and Southeast Asia, correct sizing helps FUSION’s breathable, moisture-managing fabrics work as intended. A close fit keeps the textile near the skin so perspiration can move through the garment while reducing heavy folds and friction.

For cycling and triathlon, prioritise the chest or bust, waist and hip ranges shown for the exact suit or bib category. For running and training tops, use chest or bust first; for tights and shorts, use waist and hip. Product-specific construction, stretch and UV protection can vary, so check the individual product description too.

FUSION SIZING FAQ

Questions, answered.

How should FUSION sportswear fit?+

FUSION performance garments are designed for an athletic, close-to-body fit. Triathlon and cycling race pieces should feel compressive without restricting breathing or movement, while training and running pieces vary by product purpose.

Should I size up in FUSION?+

Start with the chart for the product category and use the largest relevant body measurement. If measurements fall across two sizes, consider the product’s intended fit and contact 5DLA with your height, weight and measurements before ordering.

Are FUSION sizes the same for men, women and unisex products?+

No. Use the chart assigned to the product’s stated gender. Women’s charts use bust, waist and hip measurements; men’s and unisex charts use the measurements shown for that garment category.

What is a Tall trisuit size?+

S Tall, M Tall and L Tall follow the same circumference ranges as the standard unisex trisuit sizes but provide added garment length for taller athletes.

Are measurements shown in centimetres and inches?+

Yes. Every chart displays centimetres and inches together for athletes across Hong Kong, Singapore and Southeast Asia.
