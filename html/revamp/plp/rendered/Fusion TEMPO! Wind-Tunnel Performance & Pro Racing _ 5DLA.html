What is the Fusion TEMPO! collection?

Fusion TEMPO! is a technical racewear collection built for athletes who want performance decisions grounded in testing rather than guesswork. The range brings together aerodynamic triathlon suits, cycling apparel and selected race-day essentials, all shaped by Fusion’s rider-focused development philosophy. For athletes searching for a Fusion TEMPO tri suit, a Fusion TEMPO PRO race suit or premium Fusion cycling clothing in Hong Kong, the key difference is not simply a badge: it is how each garment is developed for a particular fit, race profile and use case.

The collection includes four distinct tri-suit families: TEMPO! PRO, TEMPO! ONE, TEMPO! TWO and TEMPO! THREE. They share a performance-first approach, but they do not assume every athlete should wear exactly the same garment. Experience level, preferred riding position, nutrition access, race distance and comfort expectations all influence which option makes sense.

02

Why does wind-tunnel testing matter for triathlon and cycling apparel?

Airflow interacts with the entire athlete, not just the label on a race suit. Fabric texture, sleeve construction, seam placement, garment fit, torso position, helmet choice and hydration equipment can all influence how a rider moves through the air. Fusion’s in-house Danish wind tunnel allows these elements to be investigated together under controlled, repeatable conditions.

Inside the facility, the tunnel’s nozzle, settling screens, turning vanes and fan system manage the airflow, while a force-balance test section measures the combined rider-and-equipment system. Because Fusion’s prototype sewing studio and development facilities are close to the tunnel, a garment can be evaluated, adjusted and returned to testing quickly. This is why wind-tunnel development should be understood as a process of refinement—not an unsupported promise that one suit will save every rider a fixed number of watts or seconds.

Professional input also matters. Fusion has documented work involving triathlete Daniel Bækkegård and aerodynamic specialist Simon Smart, founder of Drag2Zero. Their testing highlights a central principle: effective aerodynamics depends on the individual athlete and the context in which the product is used.

03

How do Fusion TEMPO! PRO, ONE, TWO and THREE compare?

TEMPO! PRO is positioned as the most performance-focused option, with a super-aero race fit, internal nutrition storage and listed UPF 35+ protection. It is the suit Fusion identifies as having been worn by both 2025 IRONMAN World Champions: Casper Stornes in Nice and Solveig Løvseth in Hawaii. For highly competitive athletes who prioritise an aggressive race fit, it represents the sharp end of the collection.

TEMPO! ONE offers an aero race fit with internal pockets and listed UPF 50+ protection. It suits ambitious triathletes who want a performance-oriented silhouette without assuming that the most aggressive fit is always the right choice. TEMPO! TWO shifts the balance toward practical versatility, combining a balanced race fit, mesh leg-pocket storage and listed UPF 50+ protection. TEMPO! THREE focuses on a more accessible comfort-oriented fit, mesh leg pockets and listed UPF 50+ protection for athletes entering the category.

Actual availability depends on the current 5DLA collection and the chosen size or colour. Use the 5DLA size guide and review the individual product page before selecting your race suit.

04

Which Fusion TEMPO! suit makes sense for racing in Hong Kong and Asia?

Hong Kong and many Asian race destinations introduce practical considerations beyond pure aerodynamics. Humidity, strong sun, exposed bike courses and long stretches between aid stations make fit, sun-protection rating and accessible nutrition storage important parts of a complete race-day decision.

Athletes who prefer to carry nutrition inside the suit may gravitate toward TEMPO! PRO or TEMPO! ONE. Those who value easier external access may prefer the mesh leg pockets offered on TEMPO! TWO and TEMPO! THREE. The listed UPF rating also varies: PRO is listed at UPF 35+, while ONE, TWO and THREE are listed at UPF 50+. These differences do not replace hydration planning, sunscreen or other protective measures; they simply help you compare the features of each product in context.

If your season includes both multisport and road riding, explore the 5DLA triathlon collection alongside the Fusion cycling collection. The right combination depends on whether you are dressing for swim-bike-run transitions, focused bike training or a road-race start line.
