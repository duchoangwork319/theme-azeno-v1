Product description.

The Ironman World Championship winning suit

This suit is the pinnacle of our TEMPO! Triathlon Range, explicitly developed for elite triathletes and ambitious competitors. After hundreds of hours of aerodynamic testing and validation at the highest competitive level, we`ve developed our fastest and most advanced tri suit to date.

The TEMPO! PRO`s innovative design features precisely constructed prototype sleeves with variable-width rib textures, strategically placed aerodynamic trips, and an advanced, textured back panel, all fine-tuned to maximize airflow and minimize drag. Crafted from professional-grade materials, every aspect of this suit prioritizes performance, providing critical marginal gains that matter at professional race speeds above 40 km/h.

Recommended for athletes aiming for long-distance finishes between 7 to 10 hours and middle-distance finishes in 3 to 5 hours. Note: Moderate sun protection-additional sunblock measures recommended for extended exposure.

Details and materials

Your questions.
Answered.

Talk to 5DLA ↗
Who is the TEMPO! PRO designed for?

FUSION positions the PRO for elite and ambitious triathletes, with aerodynamic details developed for professional cycling speeds above 40 km/h.

How does the suit fit?

It uses a close, performance race fit. Check the FUSION size guide before ordering; standard and selected Tall sizes are listed. Availability varies by colour.

Is it suitable for hot and humid racing?

The suit uses breathable, quick-drying fabrics. Comfort depends on conditions and your individual needs; the PRO prioritises race performance.

What sun protection does it offer?

5DLA describes its sun protection as moderate and recommends additional sunblock measures for prolonged exposure. No numerical UPF rating is provided for this model.

Can I carry race nutrition?

Yes. Integrated nutrition pockets provide storage within the suit. The product listing does not specify a gel capacity.

Where is it made?

It is made at the FUSION Factory in the EU using Italian and French fabrics.

What is the difference between standard and Tall sizes?

Tall is a separate length option in selected sizes. Use the linked FUSION guide and contact 5DLA for fit advice rather than choosing a larger size only for extra length.

How was it developed?

The product listing identifies development in the FUSION TEMPO! Wind Tunnel, with prototype sleeves and professional-athlete input. Individual aerodynamic gains vary.
